What changed

Designed for informed decisions

A task-focused improvement to an existing approval process.

1 · Context before confirmationPeople see the agent's name, role, workspace, and permissions.
2 · Inline error recoveryInvalid or empty pairing codes show specific next steps.
3 · Explicit human consentThe approval button stays disabled until the reviewer acknowledges the access request.
4 · Clear outcomeA confirmation message distinguishes a completed approval from a pending request.

Demo pairing code: ZIKI-2048. All interactions are simulated; no backend requests or account modifications.
